## What it is

Sky Voyage is a casual arcade endless flyer for Android. Pilot a hot air balloon through six hand-crafted biomes — meadows, mountain passes, storm clouds, desert dunes, arctic tundra and the Sky Kingdom — dodging birds, lightning, wind turbines and rival balloons while your fuel gauge ticks down.

It's built the old-school way: a custom 2D engine written from scratch in Kotlin, a ~3.5 MB download, and zero ads, purchases or trackers. Currently in closed beta on Google Play.

## How it works

1. **Fly.** Hold and drag to steer; fire the burner to climb, release to drift. Optional gyroscope tilt steering if you like motion controls.
2. **Survive the biomes.** Each zone brings new obstacles — hail, nets, spike clouds — and new scenery, generated procedurally so no two runs match.
3. **Grab power-ups.** Fuel tanks, shield bubbles, speed boosts, coin magnets and score multipliers keep a good run alive.
4. **Make it yours.** Spend collected coins in the Hangar on envelopes, baskets, crew and flags — some with stat bonuses.
5. **Compete.** Sign in with Google Play Games (optional) for global leaderboards, 12 achievements and cloud saves across devices.
