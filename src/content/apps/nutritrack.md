## What it is

NutriTrack is a friendly nutrition tracker for iPhone, built for the way India and Asia actually eat. A little crew of mascots keeps you company: Bite logs your meals, Sip counts your water, Snooze guards your sleep, Spark keeps your streak alive, Vita keeps your supplements on schedule and Stride brings in your steps. Nu, the letter-shaped guide, shows you around.

Over 480 dishes from across India and Asia, from poha and dal tadka to ramen and nasi lemak, work offline with household servings and regional names. Everything lives on your device: your account, your diary, your progress. No server, no subscription, no ads.

## How it works

1. **Build your plan.** A two-minute onboarding asks your goal, body stats, activity and pace, then gives you a personal daily target for calories, protein, carbs, fat and water.
2. **Log meals in seconds.** Search in the words you use, tap a household portion (a bowl, a piece, a glass) and you're done. Each meal slot on Home shows its own target, and saved foods are one tap away.
3. **Scan barcodes.** Packaged food? Scan it. Previously scanned items work offline, and products you add get contributed back to the open food database.
4. **Keep your supplements on track.** Add vitamins, minerals, protein powders or Ayurvedic staples from the reference library, tick them off each day, and get a gentle reminder if you want one.
5. **Complete daily missions.** Fresh missions each day across nutrition, hydration, sleep and streaks. Finish them to earn points and keep the flame going.
6. **See the whole picture.** Calories and macros against your targets, water and sleep alongside, weekly trends in Insights, a home screen widget, and two-way Apple Health sync.
