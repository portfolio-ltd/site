## What it is

NutriTrack is a gamified nutrition tracker for iPhone that makes eating well feel like a game you want to keep playing. Daily missions, streaks, points, and badges reward consistency, while an Indian-first food database — roti, dal, paneer, biryani, with regional names like "dahi" and "chaas" understood — makes logging real meals actually fast.

Everything lives on your device: your account, your diary, your progress. No server, no subscription, no ads. Currently in development, coming to the App Store.

## How it works

1. **Log meals in seconds.** Search 200+ built-in foods (plus USDA and Open Food Facts online), tap a household portion — a bowl, a piece, a glass — and you're done. The right meal type is even pre-selected by time of day.
2. **Scan barcodes.** Packaged food? Scan it. Previously scanned items work offline, and products you add get contributed back to the open food database.
3. **Complete daily missions.** Five fresh missions each day across nutrition, hydration, sleep, and streaks — finish them to earn points and badges.
4. **Respect your diet.** Set preferences like vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free and NutriTrack filters conflicting foods out of your searches automatically.
5. **See the whole picture.** Calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber against your targets; water and sleep alongside; weekly trends, meal timing, and calorie balance — plus a home screen widget and two-way Apple Health sync.
