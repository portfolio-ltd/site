## Overview

Commitline (formerly Anchor) helps you finish what you set out to do. This policy explains exactly what the app records, where that data lives, and what never happens.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect your personal data.** Commitline has no server, no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and zero third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own. Its Apple privacy manifest declares no tracking.

## What Commitline stores on your device

- The outcomes, checkpoints and if-then plans you write
- When each finish line starts and ends, and which checkpoints you ticked
- Whether you went back or spent a detour pass at the pause screen, and the reason you choose for it
- Thoughts you park, and the notes you write when you review a session

This data is stored only on your device and is used only to show you your own history and insights. Commitline does not sync it to iCloud or anywhere else.

## What Commitline does on your iPhone

Commitline uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to pause the apps and sites you choose while a finish line is open. Apple designed these frameworks so that your selections are opaque tokens: Commitline cannot read the names of the apps or sites you pick. The optional usage summary in Insights is prepared by Apple on your device, and Commitline cannot read or export it.

The Home Screen widget and the pause screen read the current outcome, checkpoints, plan and remaining passes from storage shared only between Commitline's own components on your device.

## What Commitline observes on your Mac

To notice when you drift from your finish line, the Mac app reads:

- The **name of the frontmost application**
- The **title of the active window**
- The **URL of your active browser tab** (via the optional browser extension, or Apple Events for Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, and Edge)

It never reads page content, documents, messages, or emails; it never takes screenshots or records your screen; it does not track your location. Observations are stored locally and deleted automatically after 30 days.

## Browser extension

The optional browser companion extension sends only the active tab's URL and title to the Commitline app on your own machine (via native messaging). No page content is read, and nothing is transmitted off your device.

## Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all data it stored.

## Children's privacy

Commitline does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
