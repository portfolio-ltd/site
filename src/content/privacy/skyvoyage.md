## Overview

Sky Voyage is a casual arcade game built to be played, not to collect data. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect your personal data.** Sky Voyage contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs and no in-app purchases. There is no server of ours behind the game — your progress lives on your device.

## Data stored on your device

Your player profile — progress, coins, unlocked cosmetics, achievements and settings — is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app (or using "Reset data" in Settings) removes it.

## Google Play Games (optional)

You can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to use leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves. When you do:

- Your distance scores are submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards under your Play Games profile.
- Your game progress can be backed up to your Google account via Play Games cloud saves.

This data is processed by Google under the [Google Play Games Services terms and Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive or store it. If you never sign in, none of this happens and the game works fully offline.

## Permissions

Sky Voyage requests only two permissions: **Internet** (used solely for optional Play Games features) and **Vibration** (haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not request location, camera, contacts, storage or notification access.

## Third-party services

The only third-party service in the app is Google Play Games Services (optional, as described above). There are no other SDKs.

## Children's privacy

Sky Voyage is rated Everyone and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
