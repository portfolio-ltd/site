## Overview

Sky Voyage is a casual arcade game for iPhone and Android, built to be played, not to collect data. This policy explains exactly what each version of the app does and does not do with your information.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect your personal data.** Sky Voyage contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs, no accounts and no in-app purchases. There is no server of ours behind the game — your progress lives on your device.

## Data stored on your device

Your player profile — pilot name, progress, coins, gems, unlocked cosmetics, zone stars, achievements and settings — is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app (or using "Reset All Progress" in Settings) removes it. On iPhone, this data may be included in your own device backups, which Apple handles.

## Sky Voyage for iPhone

The iPhone version makes no network connections at all. It uses no third-party services or SDKs, does not use Game Center, and requests no permissions — no location, camera, contacts, photos, tracking or notifications. Haptic feedback can be turned off in Settings.

## Sky Voyage for Android

You can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to use leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves. When you do:

- Your distance scores are submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards under your Play Games profile.
- Your game progress can be backed up to your Google account via Play Games cloud saves.

This data is processed by Google under the [Google Play Games Services terms and Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive or store it. If you never sign in, none of this happens and the game works fully offline.

The Android version requests only two permissions: **Internet** (used solely for optional Play Games features) and **Vibration** (haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not request location, camera, contacts, storage or notification access. Google Play Games Services is the only third-party service in the Android app.

## Children's privacy

Sky Voyage is suitable for all ages (rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play) and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
