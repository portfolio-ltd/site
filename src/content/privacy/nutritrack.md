## Overview

NutriTrack keeps your food diary on your device and talks to the internet only to look up food information. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and where it goes.

## Data we collect

**We do not operate any servers and do not collect your personal data.** NutriTrack has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind.

## Your account stays on your device

NutriTrack asks you to create a profile, but that account exists only on your phone: your credentials are stored securely in the device Keychain and are never transmitted anywhere. You can also use Sign in with Apple, which shares at most your name and (optionally hidden) email with the app — again stored only on your device. Deleting your account in Profile wipes all app data, credentials, and notifications.

## Data stored on your device

Your food diary, custom foods, saved foods and recipes, missions, streaks, hydration and sleep logs, supplements (doses, reminder times and the days you mark them taken), and settings are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData. So are the onboarding answers used to calculate your daily targets: name, age, sex (optional), height, weight, activity level, goal, weekly pace and dietary preferences. The widget reads a summary through a private App Group shared only with the app. There is no cloud sync.

## Food database lookups

To identify foods, NutriTrack sends **only the search text you type or the barcode you scan** to two public food databases:

- **Open Food Facts** (search.openfoodfacts.org and world.openfoodfacts.org) — packaged and branded foods
- **USDA FoodData Central** (api.nal.usda.gov) — generic foods and branded fallback

To show products sold near you first, product searches may also include your country, taken from your device's region setting. These requests contain no personal identifiers, no account information, and nothing about your diary. Previously scanned barcodes are cached on-device so repeat scans work offline.

If you manually enter nutrition facts for a product that's missing from Open Food Facts, that product information (barcode, name, brand, nutrition values — never anything about you or your diary) is contributed back to the Open Food Facts public database under its open-data licence.

## Apple Health (optional)

With your permission, NutriTrack writes your dietary energy, macros, and water intake to Apple Health, and reads activity, steps, heart rate, weight, height, and sleep to personalise your experience. Health data is handled by Apple's HealthKit on your device; it is never sent to us or any third party. Revoke access anytime in Settings → Health.

## Camera

Camera access is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are processed live on-device and are never stored or uploaded.

## Notifications

Meal, hydration, streak, and supplement reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you allow notifications.

## Health information

Nutrition targets and supplement information in NutriTrack are general estimates for adults, not medical advice. Supplement reference data (daily needs and upper limits) is bundled in the app and works offline.

## Data deletion

Use "Delete Account" in Profile to erase everything, or simply delete the app. Health data you wrote can be removed from the Health app.

## Children's privacy

NutriTrack does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
